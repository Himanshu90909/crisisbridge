# Assam flood answer evidence

The latest accessible report retrieved for the prototype is an NDTV report published on 10 August 2026. It states that flooding was active across 10 Assam districts, 28 revenue circles, and 456 villages; 137,590 people were affected; 11,933.46 hectares of crop area were submerged; and the reported total death count was 100. It also names Golaghat, Sivasagar, Hojai, Darrang, Lakhimpur, Jorhat, Karbi Anglong, Charaideo, Nagaon, and Biswanath, and reports rivers above danger level.

The India Meteorological Department bulletin issued 19 August 2026 forecasts widespread rainfall over Assam and Meghalaya during 19–22 August and 25 August, isolated heavy rainfall over Assam and Meghalaya during 19–25 August, and records rainfall in Assam & Meghalaya on 19 August. This supports an active weather risk but does not by itself prove current inundation or casualty totals.

The agent must answer: "Haan — the latest accessible report says active flooding was reported in Assam" and then show the source date. It must clearly state that the death and affected-population figures are as of the cited report, not necessarily current. For a real-time answer, add a direct ASDMA/DRIMS adapter and show its retrieval timestamp. Never convert absent data into zero.
